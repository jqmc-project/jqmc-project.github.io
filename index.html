Quantum Monte Carlo for molecules and solids, on CPUs and GPUs.

jQMC runs variational Monte Carlo (VMC) and lattice-regularized diffusion Monte Carlo (LRDMC) for open-boundary and periodic systems. Rust kernels drive CPUs, JAX drives GPUs, and the same input runs from a laptop to a multi-GPU supercomputer.

pip install jqmc==0.3.0a1

Capabilities

What jQMC does

VMC & LRDMC

Variational Monte Carlo and lattice-regularized diffusion Monte Carlo, with load-balanced LRDMC for many walkers.

Molecules & solids

Open-boundary and periodic systems, including complex Bloch wave functions and twist averaging.

Forces, pressure, stress

Analytic derivatives for wave-function optimization, atomic forces, pressure and stress.

JSD & JAGP

Jastrow–Slater and Jastrow antisymmetrized-geminal-power wave functions, optimized by stochastic reconfiguration, nodal surfaces included.

Interoperable input

Trial wave functions from TREXIO (PySCF, CP2K and more) and from Quantum ESPRESSO, converted with one command.

Runs on CPU & GPU

Rust on CPUs, JAX on GPUs. Portable from a laptop to multi-GPU supercomputers with MPI and JAX sharding.

Possible applications

Built for problems where mean-field theory is not enough

The periodic features of jQMC target systems like these, where density functionals disagree and a many-body reference is needed. Drag to rotate.

Dense hydrogen

I41/amd atomic phase · rs ≈ 1.23 bohr · ~500 GPa

Phase boundaries of hydrogen at megabar pressures hinge on energy differences of a few meV per atom.

  • periodic LRDMC with twist averaging
  • pressure from the stress tensor

Layered 2D materials

h-BN, AA′ stacking · a = 2.504 Å, c = 6.66 Å

Interlayer binding comes from van der Waals forces, which semilocal functionals miss and dispersion corrections only approximate.

  • binding curves from LRDMC energies
  • JAGP wave functions for correlation

Iron under pressure

hcp ε-Fe · a = 2.40 Å, c/a = 1.60

Iron takes the hcp structure above about 13 GPa and is thought to make up most of Earth's inner core.

  • complex Bloch wave functions
  • stress for the equation of state
